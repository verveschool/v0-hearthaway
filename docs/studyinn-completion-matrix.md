No indicative estimates were used: the source pages provide no defensible basis for estimating rates where published pricing is absent or the listing is sold out.

## Coverage and remaining gaps

- Standard 2026/27 rate published for 25/72 categories; 40 direct-listing categories showed sold out and seven Frederick Road categories were not yet applicable. 2027/28 rate published for 65/72; two direct-listing categories showed sold out and five Bristol categories have no next-year rate. Promotional and semester rates are additional pricing options on the relevant room records.
- Individual room-page photo files are mapped for 65/72 room categories. The categories with no category-specific photo page/file are: Leicester — Diamond Studio; Loughborough — Platinum Studio; Bristol — Serviced En Suite Basic, Standard, Standard Plus, Premium, and Serviced Studio Standard. Marlborough House has property-level Study Inn photography, but no Study Inn room cards; these five room types come from the University of Bristol listing.
- The 65 room source pages with image media supply 143 distinct room-photo files. Property page galleries supply 105 distinct property photos. Some image files occur in both contexts, so the combined unique gallery count is lower than the sum.
- James Street, York: property verified and marked coming soon. Its official page does not itemize room categories or publish a specific opening date, street number/postcode, rates or contract durations. Broad studio/en-suite formats are not counted as itemized categories.
- Marlborough House, Bristol: five categories are sourced from the University of Bristol because Study Inn does not publish room cards for its leased Bristol residence. 2026/27 rates, contract durations and bedspace totals are published by the University; 2027/28 pricing is unavailable in the reviewed sources. Bedspaces are not live availability.
- Lemyngton Street, Loughborough: authoritative sources conflict on building number and postcode; street-level address retained and postcode omitted.
- Walnut Gardens: an official 44-week offer labels Walnut Gardens as Nottingham, while the official property page identifies it as Exeter. The £249 offer is not assigned to a category pending source clarification.
- Room dimensions and exact layouts are not published; Study Inn says sizes/layouts may vary and media is illustrative. Occupancy is not published. No live room counts or live availability feed was found. Where the official property cards showed sold out, that captured status is represented; it is not converted into a numeric availability count.
- 2027/28 standard weekly rates are not published for Bristol. Frederick Road opens September 2027, so 2026/27 is inapplicable. For several other 2026/27 categories, the official source marked sold out at capture instead of showing a standard rate.
- 44/51-week offers exist for selected 2026/27 categories; contract length and eligibility vary. The 2027/28 listing provides weekly starting prices but does not expose room-specific contract lengths for every category.
- Some official sections show different campus travel times for Exeter, Leicester, Leeds, Bristol and Loughborough. The property records preserve the variation instead of treating one conflicting figure as definitive.
